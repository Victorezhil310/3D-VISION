Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 7, 2026 | Compliant with Google AdSense & GDPR

1. Introduction

Welcome to VERTEX 3D VISION ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to respecting your privacy regarding any information we may collect while operating our web application. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights under privacy laws including GDPR and CCPA.

2. Information We Collect

VERTEX 3D VISION operates primarily as a client-side WebGL 3D rendering studio. We do not require account registration or store personal identification data on our servers.

  • Local Browser Storage: We use your browser's local storage (LocalStorage) solely to save your custom 3D scene presets, material settings, and UI preferences locally on your device.
  • Log & Analytical Data: Like most web applications, standard web servers automatically collect non-personally-identifiable technical data such as browser type, language preference, referring site, and timestamp.

3. Google AdSense & Third-Party Cookies

VERTEX 3D VISION displays advertisements served by Google AdSense to support the ongoing development of our 3D tools.

  • Third party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

4. WebGL Performance & Device Hardware

Our 3D renderer detects your GPU capabilities (such as WebGL context support, maximum texture size, and frame rate) solely to optimize rendering performance for your specific hardware. No hardware serial numbers or personal data are extracted or shared.

5. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your location, you have the right to:

  • Access, modify, or clear your locally saved 3D scene data via your browser settings.
  • Opt out of personalized advertising cookies using our Cookie Preferences banner.
  • Request information on data practices by contacting our privacy team.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at privacy@vertex3dvision.com.
